Vancouver in code

Vancouver drawn entirely in code: every building, street, tree and boat comes from open data and is rendered live in your browser with three.js.

Inspired by isoworld's Isometric Vancouver, a hand-guided illustration of the city.

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to turn. The link in the address bar keeps your view.

Data

  • Streets, buildings, parks: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
  • Trees and 2009 LiDAR building heights: City of Vancouver Open Data
  • Terrain: MRDEM, Natural Resources Canada (OGL-Canada)
