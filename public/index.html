Vancouver in code

Open full ↗

Vancouver drawn entirely in code: every building, street, tree and boat comes from open data and is rendered live in your browser with three.js.

Inspired by isoworld's Isometric Vancouver, a hand-guided illustration of the city.

Rendering techniques adapted from Slop Toronto / Slop New York (MIT, © Mr.doob, John Fewell): the night look, bloom and tone curve, walls by what they face, street lights.

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag (or twist two fingers) to turn, two fingers up or down to tilt. The link in the address bar keeps your view.

Data

  • Streets, buildings, parks: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
  • Trees and 2009 LiDAR building heights: City of Vancouver Open Data
  • Roof colours: 2022 orthophoto, City of Vancouver (Open Government Licence – Vancouver)
  • Facades: 2022 LiDAR, property tax report (year built) and heritage register, City of Vancouver (Open Government Licence – Vancouver); wall colours from street photos on Mapillary (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  • Terrain: MRDEM, Natural Resources Canada (OGL-Canada)
