Isometric Vancouver

By @imnotchalk, original at vancouver.isoworld.xyz

This tab is a lighter viewer for the same illustration: tiles and traffic come straight from isoworld, drawn with WebGL, and the traffic simulation runs in a background thread. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
